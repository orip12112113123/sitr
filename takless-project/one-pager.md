## 🚨 The Problem

**200,000 foreign workers** in Israel (construction, agriculture, industry) face critical communication barriers that cost companies **millions in lost productivity, safety incidents, and labor disputes**.

**Current solutions fail:**
- Relying on "a worker who speaks English" creates conflicts of interest
- Google Translate can't handle cultural nuance or complex workplace issues
- In-house translators become either "friends of the workers" (hiding problems) or "puppets of management" (losing trust)

**The result:** Industrial unrest, safety violations, and massive management overhead.

---

## ✅ The Solution

**Takless** is a **Managed Layer** (not just translation) that sits between Israeli employers and foreign workers, providing:

### The Winning Triangle:

1. **🧠 Context Engine:** Our system knows WHO the worker is, WHERE they live, WHO their manager is, and WHAT their history is—before they even speak
2. **🌍 Cultural Expertise:** Not just language speakers, but community managers who understand mentality and cultural codes
3. **🤖 Technology:** WhatsApp integration + AI-assisted triage + CRM tracking

### How it works:
```
Worker (India/Thailand) → WhatsApp message in native language →
Takless Context Engine identifies worker + situation →
Cultural expert responds within 5 minutes →
Problem solved + documented + reported to employer
```

**Result:** Industrial peace, dramatic time savings, and proactive issue detection.
